## Full description (Play 4000 max / App Store 4000 max)

```
UniSphere is one space for every college community. Students, alumni, teachers and clubs from any college come together around what people are actually building.

SHARE WHAT YOU MAKE
Post projects, ideas, questions and achievements. Add photos, #hashtags and @mentions, and choose exactly who can see each post: everyone, your college, your connections or only you.

FIND RELATED WORK BEFORE YOU BUILD
Describe an idea and UniSphere shows related ideas and projects on campus, with a plain explanation of why they match. An optional originality check and writing assistant help you sharpen your post. You decide what gets posted; AI never posts for you.

BUILD TEAMS
Start a project with open roles, receive join requests, invite people, and get teammate suggestions based on skills and interests.

LEARN FROM SENIORS AND ALUMNI
Browse the alumni directory, ask for mentoring, and keep your conversations in private messages with read receipts.

NEVER MISS WHAT MATTERS
Events with RSVP, waitlists and reminders. Clubs and communities. Official announcements from the college. Opportunities such as internships and competitions. Real-time notifications, with push on your phone if you want them.

OPEN, PRIVATE AND SAFE
Anyone can join with an email address. Nothing is public: you only see UniSphere when you are signed in. Connecting colleges is optional and never counts as proof. Report anything, block anyone, and appeal any moderation decision: a different moderator reviews it.

YOUR PRIVACY, YOUR CHOICE
You choose who sees your profile, your class details and who may message you. Download your data or delete your account whenever you like.

INSTALLABLE, FAST, MADE FOR PHONES
Works like an app with one-tap access, a friendly offline screen and quick updates.

UniSphere: one space for every college community.
```
